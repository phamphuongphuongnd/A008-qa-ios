# Evenlease

A checklist app for first-time US landlords: set criteria once per rental, take applications (typed in, handed to the
applicant on the phone, or on paper), verify them with call scripts, documents and an outside screening report, and check
every applicant against the same criteria before deciding and recording why. It never scores, ranks or decides.

## Language

**Rental**: one vacant unit the landlord is filling, with its rent, bedrooms and Review mode. _Avoid_: listing, property, unit (alone)

**Criteria**: the written rules for one Rental, set before applications come in and applied to everyone. _Avoid_: requirements, filters

**Criterion result**: the outcome of one Criterion for one Applicant: Met, Not met, Missing or Needs review. _Avoid_: score, grade

**Evidence**: how sure a Criterion result is: Claimed (applicant said it), Verified (checked against a source) or Mismatch (a source disagrees). _Avoid_: confidence

**Applicant**: one person applying for a Rental, with their Application, Documents, References and Report. _Avoid_: lead, prospect, candidate

**Application**: the 7-part form an Applicant fills in (never SSN or date of birth). _Avoid_: profile

**Applicant mode**: the locked full-screen state where the applicant fills the Application on the landlord's phone. _Avoid_: kiosk

**Reference**: a structured record of the landlord's call or email with a past landlord or employer, with Attempts. _Avoid_: verification call

**Screening report**: a consumer report the applicant buys from an outside company; Evenlease stores only the key results and the file. _Avoid_: background check (too narrow), credit check

**Background review**: step 2, after a Conditional offer: individual review of convictions only. _Avoid_: criminal screening

**Review mode**: Compare (review everyone, then choose) or First-come (review in received order, offer to the first who qualifies). _Avoid_: ranking mode

**Decision**: Conditional offer, Approve, Approve with conditions, Deny or Withdraw offer, with reasons and a timestamp. _Avoid_: verdict

**Adverse action notice**: the FCRA letter required when a Screening report played a part in a Deny / conditions / withdraw Decision. _Avoid_: rejection letter

**Decision file**: the PDF record of a Rental's Criteria, Applicants, results and Decisions. _Avoid_: audit report
