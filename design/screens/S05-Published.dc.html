Rental criteria — 1428 Maple St, Unit B, Columbus, OH 43215

$1,650/month · 2 bedrooms · available January 1, 2027 · deposit $1,650 · no application fee.

We review every applicant against the same criteria:

1. Household gross income of at least 3× the rent. If a housing voucher pays part of the rent, this applies to your share only.
2. At least 12 months of rental or home-ownership history.
3. No eviction judgment in the past 5 years.
4. A positive reference from a previous landlord.
5. No unpaid balance owed to a past landlord.
6. Up to 2 people per bedroom. Pets: cats and dogs, max 2. Assistance animals are not pets.
7. After a conditional offer, convictions in the past 7 years may be reviewed individually.

Please bring: a photo ID and proof of income (2 recent pay stubs, an offer letter, or a benefits / voucher letter).

We do not ask for your Social Security number on this application. You may ask for more time or a reasonable accommodation.

We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, or any other characteristic protected by law.
