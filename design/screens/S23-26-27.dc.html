Legal & privacy

Not legal advice

Evenlease is a checklist tool, not a law firm. It helps you apply the same criteria to everyone and keep records. It does not tell you who to rent to, and fair housing, screening and fee laws differ by state and city. When in doubt, ask a local attorney or fair housing agency.
